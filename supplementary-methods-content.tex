\section*{Data Availability}
The nf-core/viralmetagenome pipeline is freely available at \url{https://github.com/nf-core/viralmetagenome}.
Scripts used to generate the results as well as parts of the results are available on GitHub at \url{https://github.com/Joon-Klaps/nf-core-viralmetagenome-manuscript}.
